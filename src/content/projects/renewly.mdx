## The problem

Small teams pay for many tools, and contracts don't just "renew on a date". They say things like "auto-renews annually unless cancelled 60 days before". The date that costs money is the last day you're allowed to cancel, and nobody owns it.

## What Renewly does

Renewly works out a **last-safe-cancel date** from each contract's renewal date and notice rule, including month-end dates and leap years. Around that: one shared list of every tool with its cost, owner and team, a renewals view of what needs a decision soon, spend analytics, and alerts before each window closes.

## How it's built

- Next.js App Router and TypeScript.
- Supabase for Postgres, auth and Row Level Security. Every contract is scoped to its organisation at the database level, not only hidden in the UI.
- TanStack Query for server state with optimistic updates; Zustand only for the session.
- Team invites by email (Resend), with role checks enforced in the database.
