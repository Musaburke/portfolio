## The brief

I wanted to show how useful AI really is when it's used wisely. A guide, not just a pile of facts, that a beginner and a professional can both read. {/* TODO(musa): who is it for first, and what made you start it? */}

## What I built

- Ten short guides in three groups: Start here, Build with AI, and Use it responsibly.
- Each guide runs the same way: what AI is good for, how to do it, where to be careful, then a "Behind this guide" area with my notes, evidence, terms and sources.
- An "On this page" list that follows you down each guide, with prompts folded behind "Show prompt" so the page doesn't dump text on you.
- One Sources page with all 50 sources and filters for how far each was checked.
- A graph of my own notes vault, drawn from its real links with the names left out. It drifts, can be dragged and lights up on hover.

## Being honest about the sources

Every source is labelled by how far I checked it: read on the source page, partly read (often the abstract), or taken from a search summary only. The Sources page shows the split, and it says that the "where AI helps" studies were picked on purpose.

## How the design got there

It took six directions to get here. Warm paper with a serif, a printed annual in vermilion, and cream with pastel green all got thrown out: too plain, sizes all over the place, or text dumped on you. The one that stuck was a dark-green-on-mint landing with a drifting constellation. An evidence timeline became short cards with the finding as the title, and three separate evidence pages became one Sources page.

## What I'd do next

- Replace the weakest source if a better one turns up.
- Check the motion in Safari and on a real phone, including the graph and the logo hover.

## What I learned

While researching, I learnt how to get the most out of AI myself. I discovered how much a second brain in Obsidian helps, how to properly manage my usage and how to prompt AI better.

I built the site with Claude, and that taught me how to use Claude better, first hand. I added what I learnt to the site as guides, so it can be useful to other people too.
